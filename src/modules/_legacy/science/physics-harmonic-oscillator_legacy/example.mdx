## Persamaan Utama

1. Periode Bandul Sederhana:
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$$

2. Periode Pegas Massa:
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}$$

3. Energi Total Osilator:
$$E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 = \frac{1}{2}mv_{\text{maks}}^2$$
